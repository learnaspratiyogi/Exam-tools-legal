01
Information We Collect

ExamTools — Photo & Signature Scanner is committed to transparency. When you use our application, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Photos & Images: Photos you capture or upload for processing (passport photos, signatures). These are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly share them.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers to optimize app performance.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous app usage statistics including features accessed, session duration, and crash reports to improve the experience.
  • Camera & Storage Permissions: Required solely for core app functionality — scanning photos and signatures.
🔒

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or photos to any third parties under any circumstances.

02
How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used exclusively to provide and improve the ExamTools experience:

  • Processing and enhancing your photos and signatures within the app
  • Diagnosing technical issues and fixing bugs via crash reports
  • Understanding which features are most useful to improve them
  • Ensuring compliance with applicable laws and regulations
  • Responding to your support requests and communications
03
Data Storage & Security

Your privacy and security are our highest priority. All photo processing occurs on-device. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • On-device processing — your images never leave your phone during normal use
  • End-to-end encryption for any data transmitted to our servers
  • Secure storage practices following Android and iOS security guidelines
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments

We retain anonymized usage data for up to 12 months, after which it is permanently deleted.

05
Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You may:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of analytics and crash reporting in app settings
  • Revoke camera and storage permissions at any time via device settings
  • Export a copy of your data by contacting us directly

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contactatexamtools@gmail.com

06
Children's Privacy

ExamTools is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

07
