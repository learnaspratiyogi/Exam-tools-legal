Our Story
Built for Every Aspirant

ExamTools was born from a simple frustration — every competitive exam, government job application, and university admission requires specific photo and signature formats, and getting them wrong means rejection. We built ExamTools to eliminate that stress.

Developed with love for the millions of students and job aspirants across India and beyond, ExamTools provides a seamless, professional-grade solution to capture, crop, resize, and format your photos and signatures to meet any official specification — right from your phone, without needing a photo studio or Photoshop expertise.

Whether you're applying for UPSC, SSC, IBPS, NEET, JEE, a passport, or any other official document, ExamTools has you covered with precision and ease.
